Method Alignment

This project follows Nesterak et al. (2025) Workplace performance measurement: digitalization of work observation and analysis (J. Intell. Manuf. 36, 3569–3585):

  • VIDEO — camera acquisition & digitalization to 1 Hz frames
  • PROCESS — activity catalogue, zones, thresholds define the model
  • ANALYSIS — motion metrics → event log → VA/NVA discovery
  • AUTOMATION — ML classifies frames → auto duty logs & value analysis
  • REPORTING — dashboards (this site) & PDF KPI reports
